# 👋 Hi, I'm Yo Kotaku!
**Yo Kotaku (JP) / Guang-Ze Yang (CN)**

I am an R&D engineer working on robotics and intelligent control systems in Japan. My background is in **control theory and multi-robot systems**, with research experience in safety-critical control, formation control, and optimization-based control. More recently, I have been expanding my work toward **robot learning, manipulation, perception, and simulation**.

I am particularly interested in building robotic systems that combine:

- **Model-based control and optimization**
- **Robot learning and reinforcement learning**
- **Motion planning and manipulation**
- **3D perception and geometric reasoning**
- **Safety-critical autonomous systems**
- **Simulation-to-real transfer**

My long-term interest is in bridging **modern control, robotics, and learning-based intelligence** to build autonomous robots that can operate robustly in the physical world.
